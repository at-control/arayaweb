Privacy notice

Updated 23 October 2026.

This notice describes information used when you visit arayainternusa.co.id or contact PT Araya Internusa.

Your choice comes first

Optional Google measurement is off until you choose Accept analytics. Reject optional keeps it off without preventing navigation or contact. Use Privacy choices in the footer to change your choice. We remember it in your browser for up to 180 days; clearing browser storage removes it.

Website analytics

If you accept, Google Tag Manager loads Google Analytics to measure page views, scrolling and clicks on our WhatsApp, telephone and email links. Reports help us understand website use and the sources of visits, including Google Ads. A contact click does not tell us whether you sent a message or became a customer.

Analytics can use browser identifiers and cookies and process information about your device, browser, approximate location and referring source. Our contact events send only contact method, page category and button location. We do not send the contents of your messages, nameplate photos, email address or telephone number in these events. Automatic form-interaction tracking is disabled.

This setup does not enable advertising personalisation, Google Signals or user-provided data collection. The Google Ads connection supports measurement; it does not itself start advertisements. Google processes measurement data on its infrastructure, which may be outside Indonesia. Read how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Changing or withdrawing consent

Choose Reject optional in Privacy choices to stop optional measurement. We remove accessible Google measurement cookies and reload the page to unload Google scripts. Withdrawal affects future collection; it does not automatically erase reports already processed by Google. Browser controls and content blockers may also limit collection.

When you contact us

WhatsApp, email and telephone links open the relevant service or application. Information you choose to send is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements. Please share only information needed for the work. These services have their own privacy terms. Our website has no enquiry form or customer database.

Hosting and necessary storage

Our hosting provider processes connection information needed to serve and protect the website. The consent preference is necessary to remember your choice and contains no name, email address or telephone number.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions, requests about information you have supplied or to discuss retention, email cs@arayainternusa.com. Include enough context for us to identify the enquiry, and avoid sending sensitive documents unless needed.

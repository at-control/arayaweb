WHAT WE DO

Typical marine project scopes.

01

Fire alarm, general alarm & monitoring

Supply and repair of fire alarm, general alarm and monitoring systems, and fire doors.

02

Engine safety devices

Safety devices and alarms for main and auxiliary engines, including Sinpol-D safety devices.

03

Engines & engine monitoring

Repair of auxiliary and emergency diesel engines, and propulsion-engine control, throttle and monitoring systems.

04

Generator systems

Supply and repair of ship generator systems, including emergency diesel generators and their controls.

05

Manoeuvring, CPP & bow thrusters

Manoeuvring systems, controllable-pitch propellers and bow thrusters.

06

Navigation & public address

Autopilot, gyro compass and public address systems.

07

Electrical panels, PLC & SCADA

Repair of onboard electrical installations, plus electrical panels, PLC and SCADA.

08

Deck cranes & deck machinery

Supply and repair of deck cranes and deck machinery.

09

AC & refrigeration systems

Supply and repair of onboard air-conditioning and refrigeration systems.

10

Retrofit of obsolete systems

Modification, upgrade and retrofit that extend the use of obsolete or end-of-sale equipment.

11

Hydrodynamic test facilities

Specialist design and engineering for marine ocean basins (MOB), towing tanks and hydrodynamics laboratories.

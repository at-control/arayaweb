ABOUT PT ARAYA INTERNUSA

Professional experience.
International technical backing.

Experienced engineering for marine and industrial systems, supported by standardised working practices and international technical expertise.

Founded in 2009 by Arif Fatkur Rohman, PT Araya Internusa brings together repair, modification, upgrade and retrofit experience. International technical backing includes HIT (Haven- und Industrietechnik GmbH), Noris Automation and ComAp.

Araya serves projects across Sumatra, Jawa, Kalimantan, Sulawesi and Papua, with branch offices in Sidoarjo and Jakarta (Townhouse PIK). We also extend the life of obsolete or end-of-sale equipment with practical, reliable solutions.

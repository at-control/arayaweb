ABOUT PT ARAYA INTERNUSA

17 years of
professional engineering.

2009Where our story began

Professional engineering for marine and industrial systems, backed by certification and international technical expertise.

Founded in 2009 by Arif Fatkur Rohman, PT Araya Internusa brings together repair, modification, upgrade and retrofit experience. International technical backing includes HIT (Haven- und Industrietechnik GmbH), Noris Automation and ComAp.

Araya serves projects across Sumatra, Jawa, Kalimantan, Sulawesi and Papua, with offices in Surabaya and Jakarta. We also extend the life of obsolete or end-of-sale equipment with practical, reliable solutions.

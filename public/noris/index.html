Manufacturer background

Founded in Nuremberg in 1925, Noris grew from rotational-speed instrumentation into sensors, indication, signal processing, and maritime automation. The German group serves shipbuilding, railway, and machinery applications.

PT Araya Internusa distributes Noris solutions in Indonesia. Discuss sensor and instrument replacement, alarm and monitoring requirements, or a wider control-system retrofit with our team.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
choose Noris.

Can a Noris sensor be replaced by a similar-looking model?+

Physical appearance alone is insufficient. The measuring principle, range, output signal, supply, pin assignment, mounting, and environment must match the installation. Send the existing part number and drawings to Araya for a replacement discussion.

What is the difference between noriMeter, noriMos, and noriStar?+

noriMeter is an analogue indication family. noriMos is an integrated alarm, monitoring, and control system. noriStar handles propulsion remote control. They address different parts of the vessel’s measurement and automation requirements.

How does noriNet relate to noriMos?+

noriMos provides onboard alarm, monitoring and control. noriNet adds remote access and cloud-based visibility of connected vessel data. Share the existing system interfaces and connectivity with Araya to review an integration.

How do I enquire about Noris products in Indonesia?+

Contact PT Araya Internusa with the model or application, equipment photographs, quantity, and vessel or site location. Model availability, compatibility, documentation, and the agreed supply or service scope are confirmed for your enquiry.
